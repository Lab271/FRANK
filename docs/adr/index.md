## ADR Format

Each ADR follows this structure:

1. **Status** - Proposed, Accepted, Superseded, Deprecated
2. **Context** - Why the decision was needed
3. **Decision** - What was decided and why
4. **Consequences** - Positive, negative, and tradeoffs
5. **Alternatives Considered** - Other options evaluated
6. **Validation** - How the decision was validated
